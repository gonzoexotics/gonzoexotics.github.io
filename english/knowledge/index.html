WHY WE WRITE THIS WAY

Enclosure conditions are not a single number.

Relative humidity changes with temperature, ventilation, enclosure volume, substrate moisture and time of day. Gonzo Exotics materials therefore describe relationships between parameters and the animal's response, rather than only a display reading.
