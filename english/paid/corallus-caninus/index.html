Corallus caninus

The Complete Guide to Husbandry and Breeding

By Tomasz Gonsior | Gonzo Exotics | English Edition

Keeping Corallus caninus means understanding more than temperature and humidity. It means knowing how the enclosure, feeding routine, seasonal changes, and the animal’s behavior work together.

This 211-page English-language guide brings these subjects together in 30 chapters, illustrated with photographs from Gonzo Exotics. Drawing on the author’s husbandry experience and published literature, it connects practical care with the reasoning behind each decision.

Inside, you’ll find guidance on:

  • Choosing an animal, acclimation, quarantine, and biosecurity
  • Enclosure design, perches, heating, lighting, and ventilation
  • Humidity, misting, hydration, and drying cycles
  • Feeding, body condition, shedding, and health monitoring
  • Seasonal preparation, breeding, gestation, and neonatal care

The book distinguishes personal husbandry practice from published evidence and explains where knowledge remains uncertain.

Whether you’re preparing for your first Corallus caninus or reviewing an established setup, this guide offers a detailed reference for more informed care.
