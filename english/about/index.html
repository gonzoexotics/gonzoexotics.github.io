GONZO EXOTICS • ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Tomasz Gonsior

Reptile keeper and author of Gonzo Exotics materials. The website grows out of daily animal care, but does not pretend that personal experience replaces published sources or veterinary consultation.

EXPERIENCE

From an interest in animals to responsible keeping

My interest in reptile and invertebrate keeping began with a tarantula in 2007. More animals followed, with snakes taking a special place, including arboreal species and Python bivittatus.

Gonzo Exotics is a family undertaking. Behind the descriptions, photographs and materials are living animals and responsibility for their daily welfare - not a stock-photo library or an anonymous catalogue of content.

SCOPE

The subjects we explore in greatest depth

  • Corallus caninus - the main specialist focus of the website;
  • Morelia viridis - biology, enclosures and localities;
  • Python bivittatus (PL) - responsible keeping, feeding and genetics;
  • Knowledge Base - enclosures, microclimate, feeding, acquisition and biosecurity.

HOW THE MATERIALS ARE PREPARED

Sources, practice and the limits of certainty

What can be substantiated

We base important claims primarily on publications, primary sources, veterinary literature and specialist references. When sources disagree, we explain the difference rather than create a false impression of certainty.

What counts as husbandry experience

A keeper's experience can be useful, but it remains experience gained under particular conditions. We do not present one method as universal when evidence and practice support more than one reasonable approach.

What we do not do

We do not diagnose an animal from a photograph, assign a gene solely from appearance or automatically transfer recommendations between distinct species.
