It all started with one tarantula

My interest in reptile and invertebrate keeping began in 2007. My first animal was a tarantula, Acanthoscurria geniculata. More followed, with tarantulas of the genus Poecilotheria taking a special place. That was the beginning of something that gradually became an important part of our lives.

My first snake was Lampropeltis californiae. It sparked an interest in snakes that has stayed with me ever since. Next came the constrictor I had wanted for so long - Boa constrictor. In time, another dream came true: the arboreal gems, green tree pythons Morelia viridis and emerald tree boas. I have kept arboreal species for years, and they hold a special place in my interest in reptiles.

The collection also includes Burmese pythons (PL). I love their powerful build and the composure they can show around people. That admiration must always go hand in hand with respect for their strength: even a large, calm snake requires responsible handling.

Today, it is our shared story

My wife and I run Gonzo Exotics together. Animals have always been part of our lives. We share not only a fascination with them, but also the pleasure of daily care and learning about their needs. Over the years, our home has housed green iguanas, brown basilisks and milk snakes, among others. As the website shows, it is still full of life - we have our own small zoo.

Behind the photographs and species names are living animals whose care is our daily responsibility. That is why appropriate conditions, health, provenance records and honest accounts of husbandry matter so much to us. Years of interest do not mean that we know everything. We want to keep learning and developing, together.

There is still one particular dream: a pair of Corallus batesii, the Amazon Basin emerald tree boa (PL). That remains ahead of me. It is good to have something to look forward to with the same excitement as the very first animal.

From one tarantula to an interest we share as a couple. That is our story - and we are still writing it.
